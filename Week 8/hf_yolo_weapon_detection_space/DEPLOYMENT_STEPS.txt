HUGGING FACE DEPLOYMENT STEPS

1. Train your YOLO model in Google Colab.
2. Download best.pt from:
   runs/detect/weapon_detection_yolov8_roboflow/weights/best.pt

3. Go to:
   https://huggingface.co/spaces

4. Click:
   Create new Space

5. Select:
   SDK = Gradio
   Visibility = Public or Private

6. Upload these files:
   app.py
   requirements.txt
   README.md
   best.pt

7. Wait for Hugging Face to build the Space.

8. Open the app and test:
   - Upload image
   - Capture from webcam
   - Run YOLO Detection

NOTE:
If webcam does not work, check browser camera permission.
